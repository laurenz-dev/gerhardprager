The Procedure

Roux-en-Y Gastric Bypass

The gastric bypass is the gold standard of bariatric surgery. In this procedure, a small gastric pouch is created and connected directly to the small intestine. The majority of the stomach and the upper small intestine are bypassed, which leads to both a reduction in food intake and decreased nutrient absorption.

Why This Procedure

Benefits of the Gastric Bypass

01
Gold standard for decades
The gastric bypass has the longest evidence base of all bariatric procedures with excellent long-term results.
02
100% laparoscopic

The gastric bypass achieves the highest diabetes remission rates of all bariatric procedures - over 80% of patients benefit.

Results

What the Gastric Bypass Achieves

Sustained Weight Loss 60-75%

The gastric bypass achieves a sustained loss of 60-75% of excess weight with excellent long-term stability.

Reflux Therapy

Particularly effective for existing gastroesophageal reflux - one of the few operations that actively improves reflux.

Improvement of All Comorbidities

Diabetes, hypertension, sleep apnea, and other comorbidities improve sustainably after the procedure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Well Informed for Your Consultation

How does a gastric bypass work?

During a gastric bypass, a small gastric pouch (approx. 20-30 ml) is created and connected directly to the small intestine. The majority of the stomach and the upper small intestine are bypassed. This reduces food intake and alters nutrient absorption. The entire procedure is performed laparoscopically.

How much weight can you lose?

On average, patients lose 60-75% of their excess weight within the first two years. The gastric bypass shows the best long-term results of all bariatric procedures.

Do I need to take vitamins afterward?

Yes, lifelong supplementation with vitamins and minerals is necessary after a gastric bypass. This includes vitamin B12, iron, calcium, and vitamin D in particular. The exact dosage is determined individually and monitored regularly.
